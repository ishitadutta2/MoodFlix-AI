Questions, answered

How does MoodFlix learn what I like?

Every chat, favorite, and reaction refines a private preference profile the recommender uses on your next request.

Does it only recommend movies?

No — every conversation can surface both films and songs matched to the same mood or moment.

Can I ask for something specific?

Yes. Name a genre, actor, mood, or language and MoodFlix folds it into the conversation immediately.

Is my chat history private?

Your conversations are tied to your account only, and you can permanently delete them anytime from Settings.
